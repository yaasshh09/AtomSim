Paired items ship together because each one is weaker alone. Tick an item only

## Why this order

The paper goes first because it describes the app as it stands and was planned
in August. After that, motion and exact physics that are cheap to build come
first, then reach, then small honest wins, then the long physics phases, and the
platform work that only matters at scale goes last. Item 8 waits on the user, so
it can slide without blocking anything else.
